# Policy decisions and approval notes

Updated 5 November 2026. These are pre-launch drafts, not a statement that live operations already exist.

## Owner direction

The owner selected **7-day returns for unused, unaltered sarees** and requested reasonable, legitimate supporting service policies. Return terms, care guidance, customer service standards and brand regulations now live in the central `lib/content/policies.ts` file and are rendered at `/returns`, `/care`, `/service` and `/brand-standards`.

## Proposed operational terms

- Seven calendar days to request a change-of-mind return, counting the day after delivery as day one.
- Complete, unused, unwashed, unaltered set with attached tags and uncut blouse piece; careful inspection is allowed.
- No restocking charge. Actual disclosed change-of-mind return delivery paid by customer. Reasonable return costs for faulty, incorrect or materially misdescribed goods paid by brand.
- No mandatory unboxing video. Missing outer courier packaging alone does not defeat a legitimate return.
- Seven calendar days to dispatch after instructions, without penalising the customer for brand pickup delays.
- Inspection target: three business days after receipt. Approved refund initiation target: five business days, or any shorter applicable requirement. Bank posting estimate: another five to ten business days.
- Proposed support hours: Monday–Saturday, 10 am–6 pm IST, excluding published holidays. Ordinary enquiries: two business days.
- Formal complaints: acknowledge within 48 hours and address within one month. Holidays/service hours do not extend these statutory complaint periods.
- Support email, WhatsApp number, business identity/address, grievance officer and return address remain unconfigured. No invented contact endpoint is published.

## Legal sources checked

- [Department of Consumer Affairs: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/E%20commerce%20rules_1732703966.pdf): complaint handling, transparency, refund handling and protection against unfair practices. Merchant-selected return/refund timings above are proposed commercial policies, not statutory time limits.
- [PIB, 10 September 2026: amendments announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=294532): announced commencement **1 January 2027**. Review the operative notification at launch. The announcement covers complaint copies, reference-price rules, search/sponsorship transparency, dark-pattern audits and National Consumer Helpline participation. Do not treat this draft as implementation or certification of those requirements.
- [Official consumer protection legislation index](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts): verify current amendments and applicability with counsel before enabling commerce.

Nothing in the proposed change-of-mind conditions excludes remedies for defective, incorrect or materially misdescribed goods, nor imposes a seven-day cutoff on statutory claims. Care instructions are general and subordinate to verified garment labels. The brand must verify its actual cleaning instructions and train support/fulfillment staff before launch.

## Internal brand use

Use the exact brand name and tagline. Heritage is expressed through architecture, textile detail, typography and restraint, never an invented founding year, lineage or certification. Product material/origin/handloom/zari claims require evidence. AI imagery and sample reviews remain labeled. No fake scarcity, hidden fees, fabricated endorsements or compulsory marketing consent. The sample crest is an original visual device, not an assertion of trademark registration or a historic family crest.
