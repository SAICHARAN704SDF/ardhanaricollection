# Ardhanari brand structure

The original owner-supplied logo is the authoritative brand artwork. Preserve its lettering, figure and colours; never recreate it with a font or generative reinterpretation.

Shared theme: `styles/brand-theme.css`, loaded after base layout and heritage styles.

- Burgundy #340509: centred masthead, navigation, newsletter, primary shopping actions and selected controls.
- Deep burgundy #260306: announcement strip and footer.
- Antique gold #B99A63: separators, active navigation underline, borders and ornamental detail.
- Light gold #DFC99F: text and icons on burgundy, with visible keyboard focus.
- Ivory and cream: catalogue, product details, forms, policies and reading surfaces. Preserve photography colours.
- Dark antique gold #80633D: small accent text on ivory for readability.

Desktop hierarchy: slim announcement, symmetric three-column masthead (service / original logo / shopping tools), separate collection navigation, campaign hero, editorial collections and products, recommendations and craftsmanship, newsletter, service footer.

Mobile hierarchy: equal side columns keep the logo truly centred regardless of icon counts; menu on the left, search and bag on the right. Shopping surfaces retain existing responsive grids, product reveal and purchase controls.

Existing account, admin and checkout pages inherit these tokens and shared components. Semantic stock-status colours remain distinct from decorative brand colours. Demo disclosures stay visible.
