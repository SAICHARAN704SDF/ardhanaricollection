# Returns & exchanges

We want your saree to feel right, from the first unfolding. Our return policy is designed to be clear, fair, and easy to understand.

- **Return request:** Within 7 calendar days
- **Condition:** Unused & unaltered
- **Restocking fee:** None

## Your 7-day return window

Request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving your saree. The day after confirmed delivery is day one. This window applies to change-of-mind returns; it does not limit remedies for faulty, incorrect or misdescribed goods under applicable law.

## What is eligible

- The saree must be unused, unwashed, unaltered and free of perfume, stains, makeup or damage caused after delivery. A careful indoor inspection is welcome.
- Keep the original tags attached and return the complete set, including the uncut blouse piece and any included accessories.
- Please keep the original protective packaging where reasonably possible. Missing outer courier packaging alone is not a reason to reject an otherwise eligible return.
- Do not cut the blouse piece, stitch a blouse, add a fall or pico, or alter the saree until you have decided to keep it.

## Customisation and exclusions

Sarees cut, stitched, personalised or altered at your request are not eligible for change-of-mind returns once that work has begun. Any such restriction must be shown before purchase. Sale items remain eligible unless a specific change-of-mind exclusion is clearly disclosed before checkout. These exclusions do not remove rights relating to defects, wrong items or inaccurate descriptions.

## If something arrives wrong

Please contact us as soon as you notice damage, a missing component, a wrong item or a material difference from the description. Share your order number and clear photographs where available. An unboxing video may help, but is not mandatory and its absence will not automatically invalidate a claim. We will assess the issue fairly and arrange the appropriate refund, replacement or other lawful remedy.

## How a return works

- Contact the official support channel with your order number, item and reason. Support details will be published before live orders open.
- We will acknowledge the request, explain the next steps and provide an authorised return address or collection instructions. Please do not send items to an unconfirmed address.
- For a change-of-mind return, send the item within 7 calendar days of receiving return instructions. If our collection is delayed, your eligibility will not be reduced because of our delay.
- Pack the complete item securely and retain the tracking receipt. We will confirm receipt and share an inspection decision, normally within 3 business days.
- If we decline a request, we will explain the reason and any supporting findings. You may ask for a review through our customer-service process.

## Who pays for return delivery

Ardhanari Collection covers reasonable return-shipping costs for faulty, damaged-on-arrival, incorrect or materially misdescribed items. For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays the actual return-delivery cost, disclosed before collection is booked. There is no restocking fee. Use our agreed shipping arrangement; contact us first before paying an unusually high courier charge.

## Refunds, clearly explained

Once approved, we aim to initiate the refund within 5 business days, subject to any shorter applicable legal requirement. Refunds go to the original payment method wherever supported; store credit will not replace a refund without your agreement. Your bank or payment provider may take an additional 5–10 business days to show the credit. We will provide a refund reference where available and help investigate delays.

## What will be refunded

An eligible refund covers the amount actually paid for the returned item, including applicable item taxes, after the discount allocated to it. For a full order returned because it was faulty, incorrect or materially misdescribed, the original delivery charge is also refunded. For a change-of-mind return, an original delivery service already completed is not refunded. Any applicable return-delivery deduction will be explained before you agree to it.

## Exchanges and cancellations

Exchanges depend on verified availability. We will explain any price difference before you choose a replacement; if the replacement is unavailable, you may take the eligible refund instead. Request cancellation before dispatch or before agreed customisation begins. Where we can stop an unshipped, uncustomised order, we will refund it without a cancellation fee. If dispatch has already occurred, the applicable return process applies.

Screens and lighting can affect colour appearance. Small, accurately disclosed variations in a verified woven textile may be part of its character, but “handmade variation” is not a blanket excuse for damage, undisclosed defects or material misdescription. Nothing in this policy excludes rights or remedies available under Indian consumer law.

# The care of a beautiful drape

A little attention helps preserve the colour, texture and fall you love. Always follow the care label supplied with your actual saree; the guidance below is general and never overrides garment-specific instructions.

- **Begin with:** The garment label
- **Store with:** Space & protection
- **When unsure:** Ask a textile specialist

## Before the first wear

- Inspect the saree, border, pallu and blouse piece before cutting, stitching or adding a fall or pico.
- Keep the invoice and care label. Confirm the fabric composition and any special instructions for zari, embroidery, dyes or embellishments.
- Avoid testing an unfamiliar cleaning method on a valuable saree. If instructions are absent or unclear, ask us or a qualified textile cleaner first.

## Silk, brocade and zari

For delicate silk or embellished sarees, consult a professional cleaner experienced with such textiles and follow the label’s permitted process. Do not assume that all silk is washable or that every metallic-looking thread is real zari. Avoid vigorous rubbing, twisting, bleach and improvised stain treatments. Tell the cleaner about contrast borders, trims and any known colour transfer.

## Organza, chiffon and georgette

These names describe fabric structures, not a single fibre. Silk and synthetic versions can require different care. Follow the actual composition and care label. Handle sheer fabrics gently, keep sharp jewellery and rough bag straps away from the weave, and avoid pulling or trimming a snag yourself.

## Cotton and everyday drapes

If the care label permits washing, use its specified temperature, detergent and cycle. Wash separately where advised, especially for deep colours or contrast borders. Do not assume colourfastness, shrink resistance or machine washability unless confirmed. Dry and press according to the label rather than treating every cotton saree the same way.

## Pressing and wearing

- Use only the temperature and steam setting permitted by the garment label. If ironing is allowed, a suitable clean pressing cloth can help protect the surface.
- Do not press directly on embroidery, sequins, metallic thread or delicate trims. Avoid prolonged heat on one spot.
- Apply perfume and cosmetics before dressing and let them dry. Prevent direct contact with the saree.
- Use smooth, secure fastenings and keep pins away from delicate or heavily embellished areas.

## After an occasion

Check for spills and stains before putting the saree away. Gently blot a fresh spill with a clean, dry white cloth without rubbing; do not add a cleaning chemical or water unless the care instructions permit it. Seek prompt specialist advice for persistent marks. Do not store a damp garment.

## Storing thoughtfully

Keep the saree clean and fully dry in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use a clean, breathable protective cover suitable for the textile, avoid compressing heavy embellishments, and check stored pieces periodically for moisture or pests. Avoid direct contact with mothballs, perfume or harsh storage chemicals. Refold with care rather than leaving a delicate piece under prolonged pressure at the same crease.

## Care advice is not a warranty waiver

We cannot promise that any fabric is stain-proof, fade-proof or damage-proof. Normal wear and accidental damage are different from a manufacturing defect, and each concern should be assessed on its facts. General care advice does not remove your rights where an item is defective or its supplied instructions are wrong.
